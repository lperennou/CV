\company~is looking for a \job~to help propose financial services tailored to each client. 
I am very excited to apply, because I am aware of the critical importance of financial services in our modern lives and because my strong analytical and communication skills are a good match for the job. 

While a PhD candidate at Outscale, I used data mining to optimize the allocation of computing resources in cloud infrastructures. 
Throughout this experience, I acquired the \textbf{organizational best practices} needed to solve data-driven problems.  
I learned to collect the appropriate data set and to select evaluation metrics before testing any hypotheses. 
%Interestingly, resource allocation in clouds bears similarities with energy trading: traders weigh risk and returns, while a cloud provider must decide when to over-commit resources despite receiving hardly predictable requests. 

Besides the technical and analytical skills, I developed a research mindset combining an \textbf{enthusiasm for collaboration} and perseverance.  
Doing research taught me not only how to communicate clearly the result of my work, but also how to discuss its limitations and embrace constructive critiques.  
I acquired this skill the hard way, by submitting my work to peer-reviewed publications until I had addressed all limitations raised by reviewers. 

Two strategic reasons lead me to apply at \company~today.   
First and foremost, I share \company's social values of accessibility for the unprivileged and transparency. 
Secondly, I am attracted by the prospect of working with colleagues who are drawn to innovation and sometimes go as far as pursuing a PhD. 
I am certain that I will benefit from working in such a rich environment.
